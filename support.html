Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Manage or cancel anytime in your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or from within the app: Profile → Manage Subscription.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Delete Account. Note this does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that separately as above.
